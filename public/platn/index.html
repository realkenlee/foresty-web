Platn

A pocket typewriter

Write now. Edit later.

Platn is a local iOS pocket typewriter. It works like a machine: you add letters, or you take them back.

Keep going while the words are here. The rest can wait.

Drafts stay on your device. There is no account, no sync, and no subscription.
