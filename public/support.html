Line and Stone

The game doesn't remember my settings
Line and Stone stores preferences locally on your device. If they've been reset, it usually means the app was reinstalled or device storage was cleared. Reopen Settings in the app to set your preferences again.
My Game Center name isn't showing
Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center, and that Game Center is enabled for Line and Stone. The app only reads your display name; no other data is collected or sent to us.
I want to delete my data
All data the app stores is local to your device. Deleting Line and Stone from your device removes its stored preferences. We don't have servers that hold any personal information about you.
I found a bug or have a feature request
Please email us with as much detail as you can — what device and iOS version you're using, what you were doing when it happened, and a screenshot if possible.
